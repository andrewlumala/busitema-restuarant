Privacy Notice

A plain-language summary — not a substitute for formal legal review before real deployment.

What we collect

To create an account: your name, phone number, and registration number. To place an order: what you ordered, when, and how you paid. If you use guest checkout: your name and phone number for that order only.

Why

To process your order and payment, generate your queue ticket, notify you when your food is ready, and let you view your own order history and resolve any issues with an order.

Who can see it

Kitchen staff see your order and queue number (not your phone number or payment details). Admin staff can see your name, phone number, and transaction history, for handling refunds, disputes, and wallet top-ups.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists, plus a reasonable period for financial record-keeping. You can ask an admin to delete your account and associated data.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything beyond running the restaurant's ordering system.

Questions

Contact the restaurant admin directly, or use the "Need help?" link in the app.
